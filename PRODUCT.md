# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. SCSS only if a token or motion need cannot stay in Tailwind. Client-side motion isolated in `'use client'` leaves. No backend/fullstack surface.

## Users

Primary visitor: a product or agency client scanning for a frontend partner who can ship complex UI, not a junior resume. They arrive with little time, want proof in the first viewport, and a clear way to write.

Secondary (not designed for first): hiring managers. The same proof works; the conversion path stays client-first.

## Product Purpose

A one-page interactive portfolio that is itself a demonstration of frontend craft: interfaces, dashboards, data visualization, and considered motion. Success is: the visitor understands what Maxim builds, believes the craft level from the page itself, and contacts him.

## Positioning

Frontend Developer focused on interfaces, data visualization and interactive experiences. The site must prove complex UI skill by being a working product surface, not a template around project thumbnails. Neighboring generic developer portfolios cannot truthfully claim this because they describe the skill; this product performs it.

## Operating Context

Viewed on desktop first (client review, often a laptop), must hold on tablet and mobile. Single-page scroll with in-page anchors. Synthetic project and chart data until real case studies replace them. Contact via email, GitHub, Telegram, LinkedIn placeholders until real handles are supplied.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page site: Hero, Capabilities, Tech Stack, Projects, Contact, Footer.
- Projects are structural placeholders (Admin Dashboard, Analytics Platform, SaaS Platform, Interactive Web Experience) labeled as synthetic.
- No backend, CMS, or auth.
- No generic AI portfolio tropes (centered "Hi, I'm…", three equal cards, Inter + purple glow, empty luxury whitespace, shadcn-default chrome).
- Visual richness must stay usable: readable type, WCAG-minded contrast, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Language: Russian (confirmed).
- Display name inferred as Максим Герасименко / short mark «Максим» until corrected

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: professional, technical, confident. Not fullstack, not backend, not intern-portfolio humble.
- Energy: bold, colorful, slightly experimental, memorable; never chaotic.
- Binding anti-goals: white empty hero, huge greeting type, one gradient, identical project cards, glassmorphism/glow as a style system.
- CTA intents (one label each): «Смотреть проекты», «Написать мне».

## Evidence on Hand

No real case studies, photography, or contact endpoints yet. All project copy, metrics, and charts are synthetic and must be labeled as such where a visitor could mistake them for shipped client work. Replace list: email, GitHub, Telegram, LinkedIn, four real projects, optional portrait.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before biography: the interface demonstrates the skill.
2. Frontend only: every claim stays in UI, motion, data viz, and architecture.
3. Color and density are craft, not decoration; every extra element must earn its place.
4. Motion communicates hierarchy and feedback; ten good animations beat fifty.
5. Placeholders are production-shaped: real structure, honest labels, easy to swap.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for text and CTAs. Keyboard-reachable nav and controls. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables non-essential motion. Semantic HTML. Language `ru`.
